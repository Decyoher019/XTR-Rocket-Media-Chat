Public Statement

Statement on Data Confidentiality for Maternal and Reproductive Health Coordination

We are concerned by reports that medical coordination has shifted to unapproved communication channels during the current systems outage. Maternal and reproductive health information carries particular sensitivity, and women affected by this response deserve clear assurance that their health information is not being handled through channels without appropriate safeguards. We call on MNCC to confirm what protections are in place during this interim period, and to communicate directly with affected women about how their information is being managed.

— Dr. Ratna Ayu
Women's Health Coalition — Banten Chapter
