Dozens Feared Trapped in Site 4 Building Collapse

By Wire Staff  ·  Updated 220926 1358H  ·  Banten Region

BANTEN — Early, unverified reports suggest a significant number of people may be trapped following a structural collapse near the Site 4 area. Figures circulating on social media vary widely, from roughly 30 to more than 60, and officials have not yet issued a confirmed count.

Emergency responders were seen arriving at the scene shortly after the collapse was first reported. Local residents described hearing a loud noise before the structure came down, though details of the cause remain unclear at this time.

This is a developing story. Banten Wire has not independently verified the number of individuals affected and will update this report as confirmed information becomes available.

Community members have taken to social platforms to share secondhand accounts of the incident, several of which contain conflicting details. Officials have urged the public to await confirmation through verified channels before sharing figures further.

This report will be updated as more information is confirmed.
