Statement on Continuity of Care for Elderly and Vulnerable Residents

Many elderly residents rely on consistent, well-understood channels to coordinate ongoing and chronic care needs. A sudden shift to unfamiliar messaging platforms risks excluding those least able to adapt quickly, and raises real concerns about continuity of care during an already stressful response. We ask MNCC to ensure that alternate coordination methods remain accessible to elderly and vulnerable residents, and that no one is left without a clear way to reach medical support during this disruption.
